# Minion datapack

Run `/function minion:give` to get a **Minion Armor Stand** (rename it at an anvil to choose the skin).

Placing it spawns the armor stand, an invincible mannequin (description hidden, skin = item name) and an invisible, invincible wolf tamed to the placer. Every tick the mannequin is teleported to the wolf and faces the wolf's yaw (pitch 0). Armor and held items on the stand are mirrored onto the mannequin; naming the stand with a name tag renames the mannequin only. Breaking the stand removes the mannequin and wolf.
